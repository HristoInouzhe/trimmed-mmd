This supplement contains the proofs of all results of the main text, grouped by its sections, together with a
two-sided bound on the statistic, the betting test in full and the proof of the parametric rate, up to logarithmic
factors, with the Laplace kernel. It also gives the details of the simulations, the data and the computations, and the
results under the Gaussian kernel. Section numbers starting with S refer to this supplement; all other numbers refer to
the main text.
